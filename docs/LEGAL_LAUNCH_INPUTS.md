# Legal and commercial launch inputs

This is an engineering launch gate, not legal advice. Do not enable paid
Checkout until the business owner and qualified counsel approve the applicable
documents and the product behavior matches them.

## Operator and contact

- Legal operating entity, business address, and jurisdictions of operation.
- Public privacy, security, billing-support, and legal-notice contact channels.
- Incident and user-rights request ownership with response targets.

## Commercial terms

- Team plan price, currency, billing interval, included seats/usage, overages,
  taxes, trials, renewal, cancellation effective date, and downgrade behavior.
- Refund and credit policy, chargeback handling, invoice support, and failed
  payment grace period.
- Availability/support commitments and an explicit statement of what is not an
  SLA during beta.

## Privacy and data governance

- Data-controller/processor roles, lawful bases, user rights, age/region limits,
  international transfers, and data-processing agreement requirements.
- Final subprocessor list and notification process.
- Retention/deletion/export schedules for identity, Workspace records, source
  inventory, AI inputs/outputs, logs, webhooks, billing records, waitlist data,
  backups, and runner artifacts.
- Security-incident and legally required breach-notification procedure.

## Product evidence required before approval

- Checkout and entitlements match the published plan in Stripe test mode.
- Self-service cancellation/portal behavior and support escalation are proven.
- Account export/deletion and backup-expiry behavior are tested.
- The public Privacy, Terms, Pricing, and in-product billing language match the
  approved policies exactly.
